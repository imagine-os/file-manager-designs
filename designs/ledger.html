Ledger is the column browser most people already know, tightened. Three columns cascade left to right, so the path you took is always visible as a trail of highlighted rows, and the width of a column is something you drag rather than settle for: here the Projects column is being widened to 230 px, with a live readout on the handle. Focus lives in the last column and moves with the arrow keys; Space opens the same file in the Quick Look pane on the right, which here renders page one of a twelve-page contract with its tags, owner and dates underneath. The path bar at the bottom repeats the location in plain words, so the screen never asks you to remember where you are.

Why it works
Columns show hierarchy without hiding it, the focused row and the preview are the same file, and every control (resize, arrow keys, Space) is the one the hand already expects.
