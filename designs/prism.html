Every tag, person, date range or status is a lens: a colored disc you drag out of the sidebar and lay over the whole library. One lens dims everything outside it; two dragged onto each other form an intersection, and the files under both are drawn at full strength with a check on the chip. Pull a lens off to the side and the table shows the difference instead. The overlap rule in the lens bar switches between all, any and only one, so the boolean logic is just the arrangement of glass on the table.

How multi-home shows up
A file's memberships are the lenses that light it, whether or not they are on the table right now. The inspector lists all of them and lets you add another; a check on the chip marks a file that matches every lens on the table.
