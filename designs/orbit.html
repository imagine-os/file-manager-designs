Orbit refuses to pick a root. Whatever you are looking at sits in the center, and everything it belongs to, or everything that belongs to it, circles around it. Center a file and its collections orbit; click a collection and it swings into the middle with its files taking the ring, the file you came from still visible on it. Hovering a satellite previews its own ring in place, which is what Atlas is doing here. The breadcrumb is not a path down a tree but the path you walked through the graph.

How multi-home shows up
A file's memberships are simply the satellites around it when it is centered. Nothing is duplicated: a file in five collections has five things in orbit, each one a click away from becoming the next center.
