Files are laid out spatially on one boundless canvas, the way papers spread across a desk, and you carve it into rooms by drawing walls. Rooms give the layout memory: Design is always top-left, Finance is always up the hall. When a file that lives in Design is also needed for the board, you open a portal there instead of copying. The portal is a full-fidelity twin marked with a doorway badge; move, rename or edit either end and the other follows. The minimap jumps between rooms.

How multi-home shows up
One original plus any number of portals, each listed under Rooms in the inspector with who opened it. The doorway badge marks a portal on the canvas and the dashed line traces it back to the room the file was placed in.
