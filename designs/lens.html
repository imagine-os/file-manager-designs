Lens starts from the observation that nobody remembers where a file is, but everyone remembers something about it. Press the shortcut anywhere and a palette drops over the dimmed workspace. You type in plain words, "pricing deck sent to acme", and the palette turns what it understood into filter tokens (type, who it was shared with, when) that you can inspect, remove or add to. Results come back grouped by what they are: the best match on top, already highlighted and one Return away from opening, then recent near-misses, then the people involved, because half the time the person is the faster route to the file. A preview pane on the right shows the slide, the path and the sharing history, so you can confirm it is the right deck without opening anything.

Why it works
It meets memory where it actually is (a rough description, a person, a moment), makes the interpretation visible as editable tokens, and keeps the whole loop on the keyboard.
