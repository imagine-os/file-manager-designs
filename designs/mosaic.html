Mosaic is for the people whose files are pictures. Thumbnails come first and come big, laid out as a masonry so a portrait render, a 16:9 slide and a Figma canvas each keep their own shape. Names stay out of the way until you hover, when a single tile lifts to show its name, size, slide count and the three actions you actually use. Filters are chips across the top rather than a form, and here two are active: only images and decks from the last 30 days. Three tiles are selected, so a dark floating bar collects the bulk actions (Move, Share, Tag, Download) and the inspector totals them up instead of describing one. The light theme is the one designers already work in.

Why it works
Visual people scan shapes and colors faster than file names, so the layout spends its pixels on thumbnails and moves text into hover, chips and the inspector, where it is read only when asked for.
