Crew is a shared drive that treats the people as part of the file system. Every row carries three things a plain list never tells you: who is inside the file right now (avatars with a live dot, so two people editing the budget is visible before you open it), what your own access is (Editor, Viewer or a public link, as a chip you can read at a glance), and who touched it last. The one folder you cannot open does not hide; it shows a lock and a Request access button in place of a date. An unresolved comment sits pinned to the contract row, and clicking it opens the thread right there, with the page reference, a reply field and Resolve one click away. The inspector explains the selected file in the same terms: who has access, at what level, and an activity feed of shares, comments and version restores.

Why it works
In a team drive the questions are social before they are spatial, so putting presence, permission and history on the row answers "can I, should I, and is anyone in there" without a single dialog.
