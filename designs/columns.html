The board looks like kanban, but a column is a context rather than a stage: a client, a type, a status, a person. You compose the board by picking which contexts to show side by side, and any file that matches a column appears there as a card. Dragging a card into another column does not move it. The card stays put and a linked twin lands in the new column, every copy carrying a pill that counts its homes; hold Option to move instead. Drag a card off the board to remove it from that one context.

How multi-home shows up
One card per matching column, joined by a dashed arc when the same file is visible more than once. The pill counts every home, including columns not on this board, and the inspector lists them by context.
